- Zubabot is a Telegram mini app with AI traders that send trading signals for IQ Option. The user approves every trade. It is an independent project built on IQ Option, not made by IQ Option.
- Money always stays in the user's own IQ Option account. Zubabot never holds money and never needs card details or passwords.
- Sign-up: open Zubabot in Telegram, tap "Start my 3 months free", then "Create my free IQ Option account", sign up on IQ Option's page, tap Allow, done. The account is detected automatically, no ID to send.
- People who already had an IQ Option account that was NOT made through Zubabot must create a fresh one with a NEW email (log out of the old one first if IQ Option opens it). Old accounts can't be linked.
- The first 3 months of Zubabot are free. If someone asks when their free time ends, give the "Zubabot free until" date from their ACCOUNT facts and say there is nothing to pay right now. Never announce, promise or hint at extra free months, and never say the free time gets extended: that is handled quietly by the team. If they ask what happens after, say nothing changes without them being told first.
- Funding: home screen -> Fund. Three ways to start: Copy signals from $20, Trade with AI from $50, Trade and talk to your AI (voice) from $100. The IQ Option deposit page opens inside the app. Any method IQ Option shows for the country (card, bank, mobile money, crypto...). The REAL account must be selected, not practice. Come back to Zubabot and it unlocks by itself. No screenshot needed.
- Welcome bonus: a first deposit of $20+ within 24 hours of first opening the app gets +500 bonus coins.
- Coins are NOT money: they can't be withdrawn, cashed out, sent to others or swapped for cash. They are only spent inside the app to unlock AI traders.
- Coins: every $1 deposited = 10 coins (+15% from $250, +30% from $500, +50% from $1,000, +100% from $5,000). Coins unlock extra AI traders in the marketplace. Also: daily gift, and Star Traders (one AI in every style) get free unlocks every month. Coins can also be bought with crypto (USDT TRC20 or Bitcoin) inside the app, but a deposit is the best value because the money stays theirs to trade.
- Tokens power voice calls with an AI. From $100 deposited: 2.5 tokens per $1. Standard voice 1 token/min, premium voice 4 tokens/min. Tokens can be bought with crypto too. When tokens run out, the call drops to chat.
- Crypto checkout (Zubabot Pay): pick a pack, pick USDT (TRC20 network) or BTC, send the EXACT amount shown to the address shown, within 30 minutes. Coins/tokens arrive by themselves once the network confirms (USDT about 1 minute, BTC 10-30 minutes). Wrong network or wrong amount = the system can't match it -> support checks it by hand with the transaction ID (txid).
- Signal Room (Signals tab): signals pop up with the pair (OTC pairs), BUY or SELL, timeframe (30 sec, 1, 5 or 15 min) and the entry time in the user's local time. Tap, choose amount, confirm, it's placed on their IQ Option account. Users pick their main AI and switch other AIs on/off.
- Trade it yourself: My trades -> New trade: choose market, time, type the amount (quick chips $1-$200), BUY or SELL.
- My trades (side menu): New trade, Open trades (live countdown, Add, Close early, Stop gale), History (wins and losses).
- Auto-gale (martingale): on by default with 3 steps. If a trade loses, the next step on the same signal uses a bigger amount to recover the loss plus a little profit. Users can choose 2, 1 or off, and see the whole ladder (every step amount) before confirming. The first trade must be small compared to the balance so all steps fit.
- Lives: every day at 10:00, 15:00, 18:00 and 22:00 WAT (West Africa Time, Lagos). Led by ROLAND, our lead mentor. Free to watch; members trade along with him in real time and ask questions. In the app: Live tab.
- Training (side menu -> Training): 8 short video lessons, done on a real phone screen: Jasmine, Sipho and Hannah. Don't mention where the trainers are from. Welcome, funding, picking an AI, first trade, auto-gale, tracking trades and withdrawing, coins and tokens, talking to AIs and lives.
- Withdrawals happen on IQ Option itself (IQ Option app/website -> Withdraw), to the same method used to deposit. IQ Option may ask for ID verification (KYC) before the first withdrawal; that's IQ Option's normal process. Zubabot never touches withdrawals.
- Withdrawing and re-depositing just to farm rewards is detected and gets the account blocked from rewards/Zubabot.
- Invite friends (side menu -> Invite friends, the home card, or /invite in the Telegram chat): every user has a personal link. A NEW person must open Zubabot with it (first link wins). Rewards count everything the friend deposits in the 24 hours after their first deposit of $20+: under $100 = inviter +200 coins, friend +100; $100 and above = inviter 5 coins per $1 (max 2,500), friend 2 coins per $1 (max 1,000). After those 24 hours the inviter gets 3% of the coins the friend earns on every deposit, for life. Bonuses at 5 / 15 / 30 funded friends: +1,000 / +3,000 / +8,000. Coins are reserved when IQ Option confirms each deposit and are paid (to both inviter and friend) once the friend has taken 5 real trades in the app; milestone bonuses count friends who reached 5 trades. Withdraw-and-redeposit money doesn't count. Coins can't be withdrawn; they unlock AI traders.
- Users must be 18+.
- Support: the headset icon at the top of the home screen in the app, or this Telegram chat. 24/7.
